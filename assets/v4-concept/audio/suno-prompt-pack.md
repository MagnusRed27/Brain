## Generation Notes

- For background music, generate 2-4 variants and choose the least melodic one.
- For active rounds, prefer steady rhythm over catchy hooks.
- For reading-heavy screens, choose lower BPM and fewer transients.
- Suno may not produce scientifically precise binaural beats, isochronic tones, or 40 Hz stimulation. Use these tracks as ambience, not medical or cognitive treatment.
- Keep final in-app volume low: about 18-32% under sound effects.
- Export as WAV/MP3, then normalize loops outside Suno if you need seamless playback.
